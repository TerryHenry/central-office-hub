Sites

Each site is one enrolled edge box, identified by its tunnel public key. Its ports are reported automatically by heartbeat (every 30s, while it's in managed mode) — nothing to enter by hand.

Console Users

Console users reach ports through group grants only — they never see the admin UI. They can connect over SSH (any username other than an admin's or _edge) or the web console at /terminal.

Groups

A group grants access to specific ports on specific sites. Add a user to a group to give them that access.
